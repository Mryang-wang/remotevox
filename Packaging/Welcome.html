RemoteVox

This installer includes RemoteVox and the T1 USB-Compatible Mic audio driver. It requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27 or later.

  1. Quit any older version before installing. Existing button mappings and settings carry over.
  2. Install and restart your Mac to load the audio driver.
  3. Pair T1-Remote in Bluetooth settings, or plug in the receiver and switch the remote to 2.4 GHz.
  4. Open RemoteVox from Applications. Grant Accessibility and Input Monitoring access. Bluetooth mode also needs Bluetooth access. macOS asks for microphone access the first time you capture audio through the receiver.
  5. In Sound settings, select T1 USB-Compatible Mic as the input. Prepare the Simplified Chinese model in RemoteVox to use on-device dictation.

If you installed an earlier test build under your home folder, move that copy out of Applications and disable its old login item.
